How to play

A traditional Nepali strategy game: four tigers against twenty goats.

Tigers

  • Move to a connected empty point, or jump over a goat to capture it.

Goats

  • Goats go first and are placed one at a time.
  • After all are placed, goats move.

How to win

  • Tigers: capture goats.
  • Goats: trap every tiger.
  • Draw: the same position happens times.
